Uncertainty-Aware Planning
Demand forecasts are represented as multiple scenarios rather than a single point estimate. D2S Bharat selects interventions that maximize expected market coverage while limiting exposure to forecast uncertainty and worst-case skill shortfalls.

5. Ek important correction: “Demand-to-Skills”
Tumne:
D2S = Demand-to-Skills

likha hai.
But tumhari actual system journey sirf Demand → Skills nahi hai.
Actual system:
Demand → Skills → Gap → Constraints → Decision → Action
So product positioning ke liye mujhe ye naming zyada powerful lagti hai:
D2S Bharat
Demand → Skills → Strategy
ya simply:
D2S Bharat
From Labour-Market Demand to Institutional Action
Tum already subtitle mein second line use kar rahe ho:
From labour-market demand to institutional action.

Ye actually stronger hai than explaining D2S as only "Demand-to-Skills."
Isliye D2S Bharat ko acronym explain karne mein over-focus mat karo.
