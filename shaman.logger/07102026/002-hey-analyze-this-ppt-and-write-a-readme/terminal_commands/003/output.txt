=== SLIDE 8 ===
INNOVATION AND UNIQUENESS
Beyond Skill-Gap Detection: From Insight to Intervention
1. Decision, Not Just Diagnosis
Traditional platforms identify missing or emerging skills 
D2S Bharat determines what institutions should teach next 
2. Constraint-Aware AI
Recommendations consider budget, seats, prerequisites and capacity 
Produces solutions that are feasible, not theoretical
3. Demand-Weighted Skill Intelligence
Combines labour-market demand with cohort-level skill gaps 
Prioritizes skills based on their potential market impact
4. Optimization Before Generative AI
OR-Tools mathematically selects the intervention 
LLM is used only to explain and communicate the optimized result 
Reduces dependence on subjective AI-generated recommendations 
5. What-If Decision Simulator
Institutions can change budget, seats or course choices 
Instantly compare how different scenarios affect skill-gap closure and market coverage 
6. Evidence-Backed &amp; Auditable
Every recommendation can be traced to:Market Signal → Skill Gap → Constraint → Optimization → Intervention
