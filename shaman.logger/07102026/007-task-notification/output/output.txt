Here are rough sizes for the two datasets. Only the O\*NET download sizes are measured; the rest are estimates. My attempt to download O\*NET and measure it unzipped failed with a network error.

| Data | Download | Unzipped / on disk |
|---|---|---|
| **O\*NET 30.3**, tab-delimited text (45 files) | **13.2 MB** (measured) | roughly 60–100 MB (estimate) |
| O\*NET 30.3, MySQL/PostgreSQL SQL | **13.6 MB** (measured) | similar |
| O\*NET 30.3, Excel | **47.5 MB** (measured) | not needed |
| **ESCO v1.2**, CSV, English only | roughly 15–25 MB (estimate) | roughly 60–90 MB (estimate) |
| ESCO, all 28 languages | a few hundred MB (estimate) | we don't need this |

**What it adds up to in Postgres for the MVP:**
- **Raw tables:** about 150–200 MB (ESCO English plus O\*NET).
- **Embeddings:** about 50 MB of vectors for about 20,000 skills and occupations, plus a similar amount for the search index.
- **Total:** roughly **250–300 MB.** That fits easily on a free or entry-level managed Postgres, and on a laptop.

The only part that grows over time is the job descriptions you ingest. Each post is a few KB, plus its embeddings.

Using the SQL format of O\*NET (13.6 MB) and the English-only ESCO CSV would keep the initial download under about 40 MB.
